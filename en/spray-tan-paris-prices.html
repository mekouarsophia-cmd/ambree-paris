FAQ

Your questions, answered

Before your session

How long does a spray tan session take?

Between 10 and 40 minutes, depending on the service. The longest, full body and face, takes about 40 minutes.

How should I prepare for my spray tan?

The key points:

  • Exfoliate your whole body the day before: it is the key to an even tan
  • On the day, skip moisturiser, oil and deodorant
  • Shave or wax ideally 48 hours before your session, and at least 24 hours before: the right timing depends on the method (razor or wax) and how sensitive your skin is
  • Bring loose, dark clothes for afterwards, for comfort and so the product doesn't rub or mark
  • In summer, sandals or flip-flops are a good idea (optional, depending on the season)
What products do you use? Are they clean and vegan?

I use professional solutions made with naturally derived DHA (from beet or sugar cane), certified vegan and paraben-free. The formulas are adjusted to each skin tone for a natural result, without an orange cast.

After your session

How long does a spray tan last, and how can I make it last longer?

7 to 10 days, depending on how you look after it. Moisturise every day: it keeps your tan going for longer and helps it fade evenly.

Will a spray tan stain my clothes or sheets?

The tanning solution itself is clear. The instant tint comes from the food-grade colouring in the solution, which can occasionally leave a mark. It usually washes out easily, but if you would rather not take the risk, use coloured sheets rather than white ones that night.

Can I shower straight after my session?

No. Keep the solution on for 6 to 8 hours before your first shower; I'll tell you exactly when at the end of your session. The colour then keeps developing for about 24 hours.

Can I go in the sun after a spray tan?

Yes, with no problem. A spray tan simply gives no sun protection, so you still need SPF whenever you're in the sun, as always.
