FAQ

Your questions, answered

Where do you travel to, and how are at-home prices worked out?

I travel anywhere in Paris. At home, the price is based on the time I spend, return travel included: €80 per hour, counted by the half hour, with a minimum of 1 hour 30 (€120). At hotels, I book a slot of at least 2 hours (€160). Appointments after 8pm or on Sundays have a €40 supplement. Get an estimate with the calculator above, then send me your request and I'll confirm the price.

Do you come to hotels in Paris?

Yes, anywhere in Paris. I bring a pop-up tent and all the equipment, so the room stays protected, and everything is cleared away afterwards.

How should I prepare for my spray tan?

The key points:

  • Exfoliate your whole body the day before: it is the key to an even tan
  • On the day, skip moisturiser, oil and deodorant
  • Shave or wax ideally 48 hours before your session, and at least 24 hours before: the right timing depends on the method (razor or wax) and how sensitive your skin is
  • Have loose, dark clothes ready for afterwards, for comfort and so the product doesn't rub or mark
Can I shower straight after my session?

No. Keep the solution on for 6 to 8 hours before your first shower; I'll tell you exactly when at the end of your session. The colour then keeps developing for about 24 hours.
