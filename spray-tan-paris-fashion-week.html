FAQ

Before you book

When should I book before an event?

The day before at the latest. The colour takes 24 hours to fully develop, so a session the day before means your tan is ready for the red carpet, the runway or the shoot.

Can I book at short notice?

Yes. You can book in the morning for a session later the same day, depending on availability. Just keep in mind that the result is visible 24 hours after the session.

Do you come to hotels?

Yes, anywhere in Paris. The floor and surrounding area are protected and everything is cleared away afterwards.

How should I prepare?

Exfoliate the day before. On the day, skip moisturiser, oil and deodorant, and wear something loose and dark afterwards.
